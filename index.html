Executive Takeaways

Strategic Observations at
Ford's Scale

These directional insights are intended to frame the conversation around operating model transformation, not to position a software transaction.

Operating Costs Exceed Licensing

Ford may be spending more operating employee service than licensing the platforms themselves. The $70.2M estimated annual ticket operating cost dwarfs the $22M-$38.5M estimated ServiceNow licensing cost.

1.2M+ Interactions Automatable

AI-enabled employee service has the potential to eliminate or automate over 1.2 million employee interactions annually. This is not incremental improvement. It is structural operating model transformation.

The Opportunity is Operational Simplification

The largest opportunity is operational simplification, not software replacement alone. Platform sprawl, duplicate workflows, and compounding maintenance overhead are the core financial drivers.

Scale Amplifies Every Efficiency

At Ford's scale, small efficiency improvements create enterprise-level financial impact. A 10% improvement in P3 resolution time represents millions in annual savings given 376,200 annual P3 tickets.

Phased, Lower-Risk Modernization

This proposal assumes phased coexistence and modernization, not a high-risk rip-and-replace deployment. Incremental rollout reduces execution risk while enabling early value capture and organizational change management.

HR and IT Convergence Creates Multiplier

Converging HR and IT service delivery into a unified employee engagement layer eliminates a structural redundancy. Separate service desks with separate tooling, workflows, and knowledge bases represent a significant hidden cost.

Important Disclosures

Analytical Limitations

This analysis is directional and assumption-based and intended solely to initiate strategic discussion around employee service modernization. It is not a binding commercial proposal, a final business case, or an endorsement of any specific commercial arrangement.

Actual platform usage, licensing, operational processes, workflow complexity, and support models may differ materially from the estimates presented here. Ford's actual ServiceNow contract terms, ticket volumes, cost-per-ticket metrics, and HR service delivery model were not available to the authors of this analysis.

We welcome validation, correction, and refinement of these assumptions through collaborative discovery. The productivity savings in Bucket 02 are particularly dependent on Ford's actual operational data and should not be relied upon without internal validation.

This proposal assumes phased coexistence and modernization, not a high-risk rip-and-replace deployment. Timeline, sequencing, and migration complexity will materially affect both cost and benefit realization.
